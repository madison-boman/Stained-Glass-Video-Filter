Digital Stained Glass

See the world like a stained glass window.

Digital Stained Glass is a web application that transforms your camera feed into a living piece of stained glass art. Using your phone or webcam, the app takes whatever the camera sees and reimagines it as colorful glass panels separated by bold outlines, creating the look of a handcrafted stained glass window in real time.

Point it at a sunset, your pet, a city skyline, or even your own face, and watch ordinary scenes become vibrant works of art.

What It Does

Digital Stained Glass captures your live camera feed and converts it into an artistic stained glass effect. Colors are simplified into bright glass-like sections while dark borders mimic the lead lines found in traditional stained glass windows.

The result is a dynamic artwork that updates instantly as the scene changes.

Features

* Live camera transformation
* Works with webcams and mobile phone cameras
* Real-time stained glass rendering
* Adjustable artistic styles and intensity
* Snapshot capture for saving your creations
* Browser-based experience with no downloads required
* Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices

Why It Exists

Stained glass has been used for centuries to turn light into art. Digital Stained Glass brings that idea into the modern world, letting anyone create interactive stained glass imagery using the camera they already have.

The goal is simple: make everyday moments look extraordinary.

Use Cases

* Creative photography
* Social media content
* Digital art exploration
* Classroom demonstrations
* Interactive exhibits
* Personal creativity and experimentation

Privacy

Your camera feed remains on your device. The application processes imagery locally and does not store or upload camera data.
